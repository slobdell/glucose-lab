name: orient
description: Orientation session for a student choosing between the glucose-lab research projects. Use when she types /orient, or when no project has been chosen yet and she asks where to start.
---

Run an orientation session following the "Orientation" section of `CLAUDE.md`.

1. Read `materials/00-start-here.md`, `materials/project-a-mody.md`,
   `materials/project-b-glucose.md` and `materials/sources.md`. Skim `notebook/` to see
   what has happened in earlier sessions, and pick up from there instead of starting over.
2. Greet her briefly and ask one open question about what she's curious about in diabetes.
3. Keep it a conversation: short explanations, then a question back. Offer to show
   something real (a protein structure, a CGM trace, a ClinVar entry) instead of describing it.
4. Near the end, summarize in two or three sentences what she seemed most interested in
   and ask if that's right. Don't push a choice. If she is ready to decide, suggest she
   write the decision and her reasons in a notebook entry in her own words.
